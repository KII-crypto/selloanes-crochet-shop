Selloane's Crochet Scrunchies 🧶

Selloane's Crochet Scrunchies is a web-based ordering and small-business project created to provide a simple online way for customers to browse handmade crochet scrunchies and place orders.

The project focuses on combining a small local business idea with a practical digital ordering experience.

Features

- 🧶 Handmade crochet scrunchie product listings
- 💰 Simple product pricing
- 🎨 Different sizes and colour options
- 📦 Customer ordering system
- 🔢 Order numbers for customers
- 📱 Customer contact and order information
- 💬 WhatsApp-based customer communication
- ⭐ Customer reviews
- 📊 Order management
- 🔐 Separate owner portal for managing orders
- ✅ Owner can confirm or reject orders
- 📦 Owner can mark orders as received
- 📈 Owner can view order information and order counts
- 🚦 Booking limits to help manage weekly orders

Pricing

The project includes the following example pricing:

Development

This project was created using Lovable, an AI-assisted/no-code development platform.

AI-assisted tools were used during the design and development process to help create, test, troubleshoot, and improve the website.

The project was developed as a practical experiment in building a digital ordering system for a small handmade business.

Privacy and Ordering

The project was designed with customer privacy in mind.

Customers enter their own contact and order information and receive an order number that can be used to identify their order.

A separate owner portal allows the business owner to manage incoming orders without exposing the management interface to customers.

Purpose

The purpose of this project was to explore how technology can help a small handmade business establish an online presence and manage customer orders more efficiently.

It also allowed me to develop experience with:

- Web design
- User experience
- Digital ordering systems
- Business-focused technology
- AI-assisted development
- Problem-solving and testing
